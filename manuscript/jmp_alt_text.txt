Proposed alt text for Journal of Mathematical Physics submission

The following descriptions are submission-ready proposed alt text. In
accordance with the current AIP author instructions, the author must verify
them and provide the final mapping as a separate .txt or .docx file upon
revision. Captions in the manuscript remain distinct from these descriptions.

Figure 1. Stokes-geometry diagram in the complex t plane showing the branch
points, branch cuts, progressive paths, and paired sectors used to construct
the two normalized sectorial solutions at infinity.

Table I. Comparison of three formal asymptotic constructions. The direct
series and transport-corrected WKB results agree in exponential rate, power of
z, and first series coefficient; leading WKB misses the transport correction
in the first coefficient.

Figure 2. Four-panel comparison of the modulus of leading Weber-approximate
ground-state solutions for two strong-drive parameters. The left and right
columns show different local branches, and the upper and lower rows show two
coordinate domains; the panels illustrate local turning-point structure but
not a proved global connection.

Figure 3. Two complex-plane Stokes-geometry panels for the leading Fedoryuk
quadratic differential. Curves and turning points differ between the real-axis
and imaginary-axis candidate branches, illustrating the orientation ambiguity
that remains conjectural.

Table II. Percentages of pixels affected by denominator flooring, exponent
clipping, and radial capping at each iteration. The largest interventions
occur on the second application for eta equal to 4, while most eta equal to
0.25 rates are smaller.

Figure 4. Four phase-and-modulus portraits of the regularized iterated local
WKB map. The eta equal to 4 panels show dense nested phase fans and a central
seam, while the eta equal to 0.25 panels show broader color fields and compact
rosettes; the latter are explicitly out-of-regime exploratory extrapolations.
