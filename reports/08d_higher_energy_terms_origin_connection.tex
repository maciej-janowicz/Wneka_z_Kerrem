\section{Displaced oscillator}

For positive drive,
\[
 H/V=\tfrac12a^{\dagger2}a^2+\delta a^\dagger a
 +\eta(a^\dagger+a),\qquad a=b-r,\qquad r^3+\delta r=\eta .
\]
Expanding the already normally ordered factors
\((b^\dagger-r)^2(b-r)^2\), and then using stationarity, gives the exact
identity
\begin{align}
H/V={}&-\tfrac32r^4-\delta r^2+(2r^2+\delta)b^\dagger b
+\tfrac12r^2(b^{\dagger2}+b^2)\nonumber\\
&-r(b^{\dagger2}b+b^\dagger b^2)+\tfrac12b^{\dagger2}b^2.
\label{eq:shifted}
\end{align}
The real Bogoliubov transformation \(b=Cc+Sc^\dagger\) has
\[
\tanh(2\theta)=-\frac{r^2}{2r^2+\delta},\quad
C=\cosh\theta,\quad S=\sinh\theta,\quad
\Omega^2=(2r^2+\delta)^2-r^4.
\]
It diagonalizes the quadratic part and gives
\[
E_n^{(2)}/V=-\tfrac32r^4-\delta r^2
+\Omega(n+\tfrac12)-\tfrac12(2r^2+\delta).
\]

\section{Order-one interaction energy}

At leading order \(C^2+S^2=2/\sqrt3\), \(2CS=-1/\sqrt3\).
The cubic vertex is odd in \(c,c^\dagger\), so its first-order expectation
vanishes.  Exact ladder algebra gives
\begin{align}
\Delta_{4,n}^{(1)}
&=\frac{18n^2+(18-16\sqrt3)n+15-8\sqrt3}{24},\\
\Delta_{3,n}^{(2)}
&=-\frac{30n^2+(30-24\sqrt3)n+23-12\sqrt3}{36}.
\end{align}
The second expression is the finite sum over intermediate shifts
\(n'-n=\pm1,\pm3\), with denominator
\(\sqrt3(n-n')\).  Their sum simplifies to
\[
\Delta_{4,n}^{(1)}+\Delta_{3,n}^{(2)}
=-\frac{6n^2+6n+1}{72}.
\]
Solving the stationarity equation sufficiently far gives
\[
r=\eta^{1/3}-\frac{\delta}{3}\eta^{-1/3}
+\frac{\delta^3}{81}\eta^{-5/3}+O(\eta^{-7/3}).
\]
The exact quadratic energy contributes \(\delta(1-2\delta)/6\) at order
one.  Therefore formal fixed-level Rayleigh--Schr\"odinger perturbation
theory yields
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\frac{E_n}{V}=-\frac32\eta^{4/3}
+\left[\delta+\sqrt3(n+\tfrac12)-1\right]\eta^{2/3}
+\frac{\delta(1-2\delta)}6-\frac{6n^2+6n+1}{72}
+O(\eta^{-2/3}).}
\label{eq:energy}
\end{equation}
In particular,
\[
\frac{E_{n+1}-E_n}{V}=\sqrt3\eta^{2/3}-\frac{n+1}{6}
+O(\eta^{-2/3}),\qquad
\frac{E_{n+2}-2E_{n+1}+E_n}{V}=-\frac16+O(\eta^{-2/3}).
\]

The next coefficient has not been evaluated.  Its quadratic contribution is
\[
\frac{\delta^3}{27}+\frac{\sqrt3\delta^2n}{18}
-\frac{\delta^2}{9}+\frac{\sqrt3\delta^2}{36}.
\]
It must be supplemented by finite-\(r\) vertex and denominator corrections,
second-order \(V_4^2\), third-order \(V_3^2V_4\) including the normalization
subtraction, and fourth-order \(V_3^4\) including its subtraction terms.
Terms with an odd total number of cubic vertices vanish by parity.  Thus the
unfinished calculation is an explicit finite collection of ladder sums.
Indeed, put \(X_{jk}=\langle j|V_3/r|k\rangle\),
\(Y_{jk}=\langle j|V_4|k\rangle\), and \(D_k=\sqrt3(n-k)\).  Its unevaluated
interaction part is exactly
\begin{align*}
&\sum_{k\ne n}\frac{Y_{nk}Y_{kn}}{D_k}
+\sum_{k,l\ne n}\frac{X_{nk}X_{kl}Y_{ln}
+X_{nk}Y_{kl}X_{ln}+Y_{nk}X_{kl}X_{ln}}{D_kD_l}\\
&-Y_{nn}\sum_{k\ne n}\frac{X_{nk}X_{kn}}{D_k^2}
+\sum_{k,l,m\ne n}\frac{X_{nk}X_{kl}X_{lm}X_{mn}}{D_kD_lD_m}\\
&-\left(\sum_{k\ne n}\frac{X_{nk}X_{kn}}{D_k}\right)
\left(\sum_{l\ne n}\frac{X_{nl}X_{ln}}{D_l^2}\right).
\end{align*}
The sums terminate because \(X\) shifts by \(\pm1,\pm3\) and \(Y\) by
\(0,\pm2,\pm4\).  Adding the finite-\(r\) derivatives of the lower-order
terms and simplifying this expression is the smallest remaining algebraic
task.

\section{Complete pullback to the Bargmann function}

Let \(z=t^2\), \(t=\eta^{1/6}x\), \(u=\eta^{2/3}\),
\(B_1=2\eta\), and \(B_2=2\delta\).  The exact covering substitution and
Liouville transformation combine to
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\mathcal T_uW=\Psi(z)=t^{1/2-B_2}
\exp\!\left(\frac{B_1}{2t^2}\right)
\left(\frac{d\zeta}{dx}\right)^{-1/2}W(\zeta(x)).}
\label{eq:pullback}
\end{equation}
The endpoint action and Jacobian are
\[
u\int^x\sqrt{Q_u(s)}\,ds=-\frac{u}{x^2}
-2(1-\delta)\log x+O(1),\quad
\zeta\sim\frac{\pm i\sqrt2}{x},\quad
\left(\frac{d\zeta}{dx}\right)^{-1/2}\sim c x.
\]
Choose neutral labels in one consistent sector:
\[
W_1\sim x^{-3/2+2\delta}e^{-u/x^2},\qquad
W_2\sim x^{5/2-2\delta}e^{u/x^2}.
\]
Because the gauge exponential in \eqref{eq:pullback} is \(e^{u/x^2}\),
all powers and exponentials multiply explicitly to
\[
\mathcal T_uW_1\sim1,\qquad
\mathcal T_uW_2\sim x^{4-4\delta}e^{2u/x^2}
\sim z^{2-2\delta}e^{2\eta/z}.
\]
Changing the action branch exchanges the labels, not the cancellation versus
enhancement distinction.  A generic continuation therefore has
\[
\Psi(z;E)\sim C_1(E)[1+\cdots]+C_{\rm ess}(E)
z^{2-2\delta}e^{2\eta/z}[1+\cdots].
\]
The cancellation is not automatic, and continuation can reintroduce the
second component.

\section{Connection conjecture}

\begin{conjecture}[Global compensated-to-essential coefficient]
There is a finite physical canonical domain chain and normalized endpoint
bases such that
\[
\mathcal T_uW_1^{(j)}\sim1,\qquad
\mathcal T_uW_2^{(j)}\sim z^{2-2\delta}e^{2\eta/z},
\]
and
\[
\mathcal A_{\Gamma_{\rm phys}}W_1^{(0)}
=C_1W_1^{(m)}+C_{\rm ess}W_2^{(m)}.
\]
With the displayed Wronskian order,
\[
\boxed{C_{\rm ess}=
\frac{\Wr_\zeta(\mathcal A_{\Gamma_{\rm phys}}W_1^{(0)},W_1^{(m)})}
{\Wr_\zeta(W_2^{(m)},W_1^{(m)})}.}
\]
Physical symmetries reduce holomorphic patching to \(C_{\rm ess}=0\).
Its zeros with multiplicities equal those of
\(\Delta_{\rm WI}(2\eta,2\delta,-2E/V,2\eta)\); locally
\(C_{\rm ess}=G\Delta_{\rm WI}\), with \(G\) holomorphic and nonzero.
Near a fixed level,
\[
C_{\rm ess}=\frac{A}{\Gamma[-p_u(E)]}+R,\qquad A\ne0,\qquad
R=O(u^{-1}A).
\]
\end{conjecture}
Every existence, equivalence, symmetry-reduction, and error assertion in this
conjecture remains unproved.  Nonzero basis rescalings multiply
\(C_{\rm ess}\) by a nonzero factor and preserve its zero set.  Formula
\eqref{eq:energy} has an independent formal operator derivation and does not
prove the conjecture.
